# Spaces tree

The `nuoh` layout's Spaces card, in place of Herdr's list. Each repository's main checkout carries every worktree under it, open as a space or not, drawn as a graph (a rail in the space's color with a node per worktree) or as blocks. The person gathers repositories and loose spaces into colored folders and pins the ones they want on top. Another mod can put a second line under a worktree, and a tab dragged from the tab bar onto a row moves there.

![Spaces tree](preview.png)

## What you see

- **The card,** on top of the sidebar: the layout node's `surface` `#161616`, 14px corners, 1px `#FFFFFF12` line and `md` shadow; the mod paints no fill. The kit's 40px `cardHeader` carries a 14px `layers`, `Spaces` in 13px medium `muted` and the count of spaces in 12px `dim`, then three 28px ghost buttons: the style (`layers`, `Show as blocks`, or `git-branch`, `Show as a graph`), `folder-plus` (`New folder`) and `plus` (`New space`). The list sits 8px in from the sides with 8px under it, its scrollbar always shown.
- **Head rows,** a repository's main checkout or a space outside a repository: 36px, or 48px when the branch isn't `main` or `master`, which goes on a second 11px `unknown` `#707070` line cut with an ellipsis. Each has a 22px avatar with 6px corners (the first letter in bold 12px white on the space's color, one of `#22a99a #e3814f #5b8def #8b7cf6 #3fae6a #d9649a`), the name in 13px medium (`foreground` when open, `unknown` when closed), a 12px `pin` on a pinned row, `↑2 ↓1` in 11px `unknown`, a 7px dot in its status and, on a repository with worktrees, a fold chevron. Hovering a repository's row swaps its counts for `+` (`New worktree`); a folded one shows how many worktrees it has and the most urgent status in it.
- **Graph:** the worktrees under their repository, open ones first, then closed ones by their branch's latest commit. Rows are 34px, 52px in, on a 1.5px rail in the space's color at `B3`: an open worktree's 10px node is filled in its status color, a closed one's is a hollow ring. The active worktree's node becomes a 14px ring filled with the group's ground around a 6px dot (its status color while working, blocked or done, else the space's color), the rail runs in full color down to it, and its name is `foreground`; other open names are `muted`, closed ones `unknown`. Hovering a worktree shows a box in `hover` with 7px corners from the middle of its node to the right edge, 2px in from top and bottom.
- **The active group** (the space, or the repository holding it) sits on `raised` `#1C1C1C` (`selected` inside a folder) with 8px corners, a 1px `dim` `#505050` frame and 6px under its last worktree.
- **Blocks:** each entry a block with 10px corners, 6px apart, on `surface` with a `border` line (`chrome` inside a folder, the last one 6px from the card's edge), the active one on `raised` with a `dim` line; the head row carries a 3px status bar on its left, then under a 1px line come the worktrees, 40px in, each open one with its own bar.
- **A second line:** a worktree whose path has an entry in shared `spaces-tree.lines` gets a 48px row, with the entry's `text` in 11px in its `tone` color (`working`, `blocked`, `done`, `accent`, else `unknown`), then `· <why>` in `muted`, cut with an ellipsis.
- **Order:** pinned repositories and spaces first, then the folders (pinned ones first, then in the order the person dragged them into), then everything else in Herdr's order.
- **Folders:** a card on `surface` with a 1px `border` line, 10px corners, 4px of room inside and 6px between cards, opened by a 26px band on `raised` (6px corners, 8px sides): a 12px `folder` icon in the folder's color, its name in 13px bold `foreground`, a `pin` when pinned, how many repositories and spaces it holds in 11px `unknown`, and a 12px chevron. The band turns `selected` on hover. A folded card is only its band, which says `N need you` when its agents are blocked or done and adds a dot in its most urgent status; an open one holds its entries 8px in from the card's edge. Its line turns `accent` while a row is held over the band, and `dim` when folded around the active space.
- **Popups:** `Move to folder…` (240px): `MOVE TO FOLDER · <NAME>`, the folders with a dot in their color and an `accent` check on the current one, `New folder…`, and under a line `Remove from folder` when it is in one. A folder's right-click (220 x 157): six 22px colors, the current one ringed in `foreground`, `Pin folder` or `Unpin folder`, `Rename…` and `Delete folder` in `blocked`. The name dialog (340 x 180): `New folder` or `Rename folder` with `Esc`, a `Folder name` field, the six colors, Cancel (a secondary button) and Create or Save (primary), which does nothing while the name is empty.
- **The folded sidebar** (under 160px): a 31px header with only `+`, then 26px rows: the pinned spaces' numbers (their place in Herdr's order), each folder as a 16px tile in its color followed by its spaces' numbers while it is open, then the rest. The active number is `foreground` on `raised`, the others `muted`; blocked or done adds a 5px dot, to a folded folder's tile too. No pins, branches or second lines.

## How it behaves

- **Clicks:** a space's row runs `space.select`; a closed worktree's runs `space.new { folder, machine: "" }`, at most once in 3 s per path. `+` opens Herdr's New worktree dialog for the repository, the chevron folds it (shared `spaces.folded`), the style button switches graph and blocks (shared `spaces.style`), as `herdr-app do nuoh-spaces-tree.style` does.
- **Right-click** on a row with a space opens nuoh's copy of Herdr's space menu, with `Pin to top` (or `Unpin`) and `Move to folder…` above Close; a worktree's menu pins or files its whole repository.
- **Dragging a row:** press a head row and let go on another head row of the same machine, on the same side of the pinned line, and the space moves there (`space.move`), the held row at half opacity and a 2px `accent` line on the side it lands; it also joins that row's folder, or leaves its own. Let go on a folder's band and it files there; let go on itself and it opens. A pinned row never goes into a folder by dragging.
- **Folders:** a click on the band folds it. A band let go on another band of the same side of the pinned line moves the folder after it (moved down) or before it (moved up), its card at half opacity and the line along the edge it lands next to. The popups recolor at once, pin the folder above the unpinned ones, rename through the dialog and delete, which gives the folder's repositories and spaces back without closing any. The header's folder button makes an empty folder.
- **Following:** when the active space changes from anywhere (a key, the palette, a toast, `agent.next`), its folder opens unless the row is pinned, and the list scrolls just enough to show its card or repository; folding it again while that space stays in front keeps it folded. A space picked in the tree leaves the list where it is.
- **Git:** for each local space's folder, `git rev-parse --show-toplevel --path-format=absolute --git-common-dir`; for each repository, `git worktree list --porcelain` and `git for-each-ref` (branches, ahead and behind, commit dates), then a `watchFolder` on its git folder. It reads again when an agent in it stops working or a program in front exits, and, while the window is active, every place unread for 60 s outside folded repositories and folded folders. A new space waits up to 1.5 s for git before it shows.
- **Colors:** each space gets the least used of the six colors, kept in shared `space.colors` for the top bar and the Agents card; the home space `~` gets none and shows the first.
- **Tab drops** are described in [`mods/move-tab/RECIPE.md`](../move-tab/RECIPE.md).

## Built from

- `recipes/spaces-tree.ts` points the `spaces` block at `spaces-tree` (installed as `nuoh-spaces-tree`) and patches `nuoh-menu`'s space case: when the props carry `folder`, `Pin to top` or `Unpin` (Herdr's `mods/workspace-chrome/pin.svg` and `pin-off.svg`) writes `folder.pins` to shared `spaces.pinned`, and `Move to folder…` (`folder.svg`) opens the tree as a 240px popup with `{ ...folder, role: "folders" }`. `recipes/cards.ts` makes the block a card.
- `main.js` holds the view's state, its git reads and `render`; `rows.js` the graph and block rows and the folder cards; `rail.js` the folded sidebar; `actions.js` the clicks, menus and drops, each a class the view extends; `tree.js` and `folders.js` the pure logic; `popups.js` the three overlays the mod opens of itself by `props.role` (`folders`, `folder`, `name`). `lib/checkouts.js` reads worktree lists and finds a folder's checkout, `lib/space-colors.js` holds the colors and `lib/layout-tree.js` the folded check.
- Shared state: `spaces.folders` is `{ folders: [{ id, name, color, folded, pinned }], members: { <git common dir or space id>: <folder id> } }` in the person's order; `spaces.pinned` a list of the same keys. Another mod writes the second lines as one map, `setShared("spaces-tree.lines", { "/path/to/worktree": { text: "PR open", tone: "working", why: "2 checks running" } })`; nothing in this repo writes it.

## Watch out

- A folder's member is the tree's entry: a repository by its git common dir, so its worktrees go with it, and a loose space by its id. A space git hasn't answered for yet shows as a loose row, so right after the window opens a repository can sit outside its folder for a moment. A loose space that closes leaves its id behind, and a folder holding only such ids shows empty until deleted.
- A folder's card and a repository are one child each of the list, so scrolling brings the card or the repository into view, not the row inside it; only the folded sidebar has one child per space.
- `Move to folder…` shows only when the tree opens the menu, since only the tree knows the row's repository; the popup's height is worked out then, from the number of folders. `Pin to top` lives in `nuoh-menu`, which can't read the tree's logic, so the tree hands it the pins as they would be after the click.
- A band keeps one `on_mouse_up`, which files a held row first and folds when it was pressed and let go on itself, and no `on_click`, so a click folds once. A folder let go on another band never reaches that band's `on_mouse_up` in the window (Herdr 0.1.16): the list's own `on_mouse_up` moves it to the band the pointer was last over, and leaving a band forgets it, so the line and the drop agree.
- A pinned repository keeps its folder in `spaces.folders`, and a pin on a closed repository stays, so it comes back pinned when a space opens it again.

## Done when

- `bun run apply-layout` installs `nuoh-spaces-tree`, the `spaces` block shows a repository's worktrees under it, and `herdr-app status` shows no errors.
- The style button switches between graph and blocks, and the active worktree shows its ring in both.
- Setting `spaces-tree.lines` to `{ "<worktree path>": { "text": "Ready", "tone": "done", "why": "ready to merge" } }` shows that text and why under the worktree's name.
- Right-click a space › `Move to folder…` › `New folder…` makes a folder with that space in it; dragging a repository's row onto its band files the repository with its worktrees; a click folds it and shows `N need you`; going to a space inside opens it; `Delete folder` gives everything back.
- Dragging a folder's band onto another moves it, after when moved down and before when moved up; `Pin to top` on a repository in a folded folder shows it at the top with its pin; `Pin folder` puts the folder first; neither drag crosses the pinned line.
- Folding the sidebar shows the numbers and folder tiles, and a click on a tile folds that folder.

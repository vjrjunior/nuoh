# Move tab

A tab belongs to the space it was opened in. When work starts in one checkout of a repo and goes on in another, Move tab sends the tab there with its Claude Code conversation resumed. Right-click a tab and pick `Move to…` (or press `cmd-shift-m` for the focused tab) and pick a checkout, or drag the tab onto a row of the Spaces tree, and a moment later the same conversation runs in that checkout's folder, in its space, with the old space left behind. A Claude session can also schedule its own move from its terminal: when its turn ends, its pane goes there and the resumed Claude starts on the prompt it was given. It needs no hooks and edits no shell startup files.

## What you see

- **The menu item:** `Move to…` with the `git-branch` icon just above `Close` in a tab's right-click menu, with the key bound to it on its right.
- **The popup,** 300 x 320px at the pointer on `popover`, 5px padding: a `Move to` heading, then a row per target (at least 32px, 8px sides, `git-branch` in `muted`, the label in `foreground` and, when it differs, a detail at `TEXT.sm` in `muted`). The first row is on `selected`; ↑ ↓ move the selection, wrapping, Enter picks, Escape closes. While it reads the checkouts it says `Looking for checkouts…`, and with nothing to pick `No other checkout of this repo.`
- **The targets,** in this order: the main checkout, as each of its spaces (`main checkout · open`) or, with none, a row named after its folder (`main checkout`); every space open in a worktree (`<branch> · open`); then the worktrees with no space, by branch name. The tab's own space is never a row, and neither is a space on another machine.
- **Dragging:** once the pointer has moved 4px with a tab held, the tab fades to 0.45, every Spaces tree row that would take it gets a dashed 1px `accent` border following its corners, and the row under the pointer a solid one; along the tab bar, the tab it would land on gets the `accent` border instead. The pointer is a closed hand over the tab bar and the Spaces tree.
- **A new space** for a worktree is named after its branch: the part after the last `--`, else after the last `/`, a leading ticket id upper-cased and followed by ` · ` and the rest with dashes and underscores as spaces (`feat/ABC-4825--abc-5365-bulk-orders-cron` is `ABC-5365 · bulk orders cron`, `feat/move-to-worktree` is `move to worktree`). A space for the main checkout or a detached worktree gets Herdr's name; an existing space is never renamed.
- **Toasts:** `Moved to <branch or folder name>` (success, 4 s), with the body `Claude's background jobs ended.` when a Claude was ended or `Claude had no conversation yet, so a new one started.`; `Can't move this tab` (warning) with the reason, when nothing changed; `Couldn't move the tab` (error) when the move broke off.

## How it behaves

- **Asking.** The popup takes the tab from its props (the menu passes it) or from `focused()`. It, or a drop on the Spaces tree, writes shared `move-tab.request` (`{ id, tab, space, to, toSpace, at }`) and closes. The worker, an invisible floating copy, clears the request, drops it after 10 s, and runs one move at a time (`Another move is running.`), so a move outlives the popup that asked for it.
- **The drag.** Pressing a tab writes shared `move-tab.drag` (`{ tab, space, at }`), and moving 4px adds `moving: true`; the tab bar clears it on its own release, after its own select or reorder. A Spaces tree row takes it while it is under 30 s old, when the row has a checkout of the same repo (git common dir) and isn't the drag's own space; the row lights from `on_hover` and `on_mouse_move`, and a release on a lit row writes the request with the row's checkout and space. Any press anywhere ends a drag older than 300 ms, so a later click on a row only opens it. The folded sidebar takes no drops.
- **What moves,** pane by pane. A Claude is ended with `kill -TERM` and started again as `claude --resume <id>` with the options it started with and, when the checkout changes, `--append-system-prompt "This session moved from <old> (branch <name>) to <new> (branch <name>). Work in <new> from now on; files under <old> belong to another checkout."`. A shell with nothing running gets a new shell. A mod pane opens again with its props. Each terminal starts in the matching folder: its folder relative to its checkout's root, under the target's root (`src/app` stays `src/app`), or the target's root when that folder isn't there.
- **The conversation** is the live `sessionId` in `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json` of the Claude in front. The options kept are Comeback's plus `--settings`, `--mcp-config` and `--plugin-dir` (`OPTIONS` and `LOADS_CODE` in `lib/claude.js`), with relative paths in `--add-dir`, `--plugin-dir`, and `--mcp-config` or `--settings` unless inline JSON, resolved against the old folder. A Claude with no turns has no transcript in `~/.claude/projects/` yet, so a new `claude` with those options starts in its place.
- **The order.** Every pane is read before anything is touched: the tab's splits from Herdr's document, then each terminal with `hc get`; a refusal happens here. The checkouts come from `git worktree list --porcelain`. Each Claude is read once more, signalled, and polled with `ps -p` every 250 ms until it is gone, up to 10 s. The target space is the one asked for, else an open space of the target checkout, else a new one (`space.new { folder, name }`). The new tab follows the old tab's splits (`tab.new`, then `pane.focus` and `terminal.open` per split, `pane.split` for a mod pane), named as the old tab when the whole tab moves. The old tab closes; when it was its space's only tab, the space first gets a shell tab at its checkout's root. The target space and the new tab are selected.
- **Scheduled from a terminal.** `sh ~/.config/herdr-app/mods/nuoh-move-tab/schedule.sh --to <worktree> [--prompt <text>]`, run inside a Claude session, checks at once and prints one line. It labels `$HC_TERMINAL_ID` with `vjr.move-to={"v":1,"to":…,"session":…,"at":<ms>,"prompt"?:…}` and exits 0, or exits 1 with the reason and no label: outside a Herdr terminal, with `hc`, `jq`, `herdr-app` or `git` missing, when `herdr-app status` shows a layout other than `nuoh`, when Claude isn't in front, is a sub-agent or reports no status, with no conversation id, or when the destination isn't another worktree of the repo, or the prompt starts with `-`. Without `--to` it prints its usage and exits 2; `--cancel` clears the label.
- **Running a scheduled move.** The worker watches local terminals with that label, and once the agent has been `idle` or `done` for 2 s it clears the label first, so a move cut off never runs twice, then moves only that pane: other panes of the tab, a dev server say, stay, and a Claude alone in its tab moves the whole tab. The prompt is the resumed Claude's last argument, so it starts on that turn. The view follows only when that tab was on screen. A label that can't be read, waited 4 hours or can't be cleared is dropped with a toast, and a conversation that changed since (`/clear`) refuses.
- **When it fails** after a Claude ended, the toast gives `cd <folder> && claude --resume <id> <options>` for each Claude that wasn't started again, and its click opens a new tab in the old space that resumes the first. A Claude whose start was asked for is never offered, since Herdr may have started it before the error: the toast says it may already be running. A failure after the new tab is built, splits included, is a warning, `Moved the tab`.

## Built from

- `recipes/move-tab.ts` copies `mods/move-tab/`, Herdr's `menu` and Herdr's `tabs` (`nuoh-menu` and `nuoh-tabs`, the copies other recipes patch too). In the menu it adds `["Move to…", "move-tab", { tab, space }]` before `Close` in the tab case, opens it with `openOverlay({ mod: "move-tab", kind: "popup", width: 300, height: 320, props })`, gives it `git-branch` and the key bound to `mod.open:nuoh-move-tab`. In the tab bar, a tab's press writes the drag and keeps its position, the bar's `on_mouse_move` sets `moving` past 4px and shows `cursor_grabbing()`, the bar's release clears the drag, each tab gains the faded and outlined looks and an `on_hover`, and a tab's release reorders the bar only while the drag is set. It adds the worker, `{ "id": "move-tab", "content": { "mod": "move-tab", "props": { "role": "worker" } }, "edge": "left", "align": "end" }`, to the root's floating blocks and binds `cmd-shift-m` to `mod.open:nuoh-move-tab` in `layout:nuoh`; `mod.json`'s 300 x 320 `overlay` is how that key opens the popup.
- `main.js` is the popup, or the worker with `props.role: "worker"`; `logic.js` holds the pure parts (reasons, the schedule, the prompt box, the targets, the splits, the space name); `move.js` the move, with every effect behind an `io` object; `io.js` the real one; `schedule.sh` the POSIX `sh` script, installed with the mod without its executable bit, hence `sh`. `lib/claude.js` and `lib/checkouts.js` are shared with Comeback and the Spaces tree, whose `tree.js` and `actions.js` take the drop.
- It runs `git`, `hc get`, `hc label`, `hc read`, `ps`, `kill`, `test`, `herdr-app do` for every change (including `terminal.select` and `terminal.clear_selection` at Claude's cursor), and `sh` only to pipe `doc.get` into `hc connect`; `schedule.sh` runs `hc`, `jq`, `git` and `herdr-app status`. It reads `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json`, lists `~/.claude/projects/`, never writes Herdr's document and never reaches the network.

## Watch out

- **Refusals** (`where` is `this tab`, or `pane 2` in a split tab): Claude is working or waiting for you; there's unsent text in Claude's prompt (the toast quotes its first 40 characters); `<Program> is running in <where>.` for anything but Claude, an idle shell or a mod; Claude has sub-agents, or is another agent's sub-agent; its conversation can't be found; the tab is on another machine, not in a git repo, already there or gone; the checkout is gone.
- **A draft and a suggestion look the same as text.** Before ending a Claude, the worker finds its input box on screen and, when the box has text, moves a selection to the program's cursor: right after `❯ ` it is Claude's grey suggestion, further on a draft. Any selection the person had in that terminal is cleared, and a draft whose cursor was moved back to the start reads as a suggestion.
- **Two Claudes never share a conversation:** the new one starts only after the old process is gone, and a Claude that doesn't exit in 10 s stops the move. Claude's background processes and MCP servers end with it.
- **A Claude that is its terminal's own process** takes its pane, and its tab, with it; when that tab is its space's only one, the space gets its shell tab before the signal, since Herdr closes a space with its last tab.
- Splits keep their shape, not their sizes. An original `--append-system-prompt` isn't kept. A move between two spaces of one checkout adds no note.
- The transcript stays under the first folder's project in `~/.claude/projects/`: `claude --resume <id>` works anywhere, but the worktree's own `/resume` picker may not list it.
- A move cut off by a layout switch or a restart isn't run again and gives no toast.
- A tab let go over a terminal or a panel stays in the drag, faded with its rows dashed, until the next press, for at most 30 s.
- Only `nuoh`'s worker reads the label, which is why the script refuses when another layout shows; a label written then waits for `nuoh`, for up to 4 hours. A Claude that reports no status never reads as idle, so the script refuses it.
- `apply-layout` rewrites `layout:nuoh` on every run: change `cmd-shift-m` in `recipes/move-tab.ts`.

## Done when

- `herdr-app check` passes on `nuoh-move-tab`, and `bun test` passes.
- Right-clicking a tab shows `Move to…` above `Close` with `cmd-shift-m` on its right, and the popup lists the repo's other checkouts without the tab's own space; `cmd-shift-m` opens it for the focused tab.
- A Claude tab moved from the main checkout to a worktree shows up in that worktree's space; asked `pwd`, Claude answers the worktree, and the conversation goes on. A Claude that never had a turn moves as a new Claude, and the toast says so.
- The old space no longer has the tab; when it was its only one, it has a shell at the checkout's root.
- A tab running `python3 -m http.server 8765` refuses with `Python is running in this tab.`; a working Claude refuses with `Claude is working in this tab. Move it when the turn ends.`; `hi` typed and not sent refuses and stays in the prompt, while a fresh Claude showing only `Try "…"` moves.
- Dragging a Claude tab dashes the worktree rows that take it and outlines the one under the pointer; releasing there moves it. A click on a tab never fades it, the tab's own space never lights, and after a tab is let go over a terminal a click on a worktree row only opens it.
- In a Claude session, `schedule.sh --to <a worktree> --prompt "Say pwd"` prints the move; when the turn ends the conversation shows up in that worktree's space and answers with its path, while a dev server split beside it stays. With another layout on screen, the script exits 1 and names it.
